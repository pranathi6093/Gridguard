GridGuard

Autonomous AI Workforce for Rural Transformer Reliability

TransformerGuard is an AI-based maintenance workforce designed to help DISCOMs detect hidden transformer failure risks before breakdowns occur.

Instead of only checking whether a transformer is currently working, TransformerGuard analyzes load and current patterns over time to identify abnormal behaviour and estimate failure risk.

Problem

Rural transformers can develop hidden overload stress before suddenly failing.

A transformer may still be supplying power and appear healthy, while repeated overload patterns gradually increase its risk of failure.

When the transformer finally fails, it can cause:

- Unexpected power outages
- Expensive emergency repairs
- Irrigation disruption
- Loss of productive time for farmers

💡 Our Solution

TransformerGuard continuously analyzes existing transformer data and acts as an AI maintenance workforce.

It:

1. Monitors transformer load/current data
2. Learns normal transformer behaviour
3. Detects abnormal overload patterns
4. Predicts transformer failure risk
5. Identifies possible stress spreading to nearby transformers
6. Prioritizes which transformer should receive maintenance first
7. Helps maintenance teams intervene before failure

 Key USP

Beyond Individual Transformers
A transformer does not operate in isolation.

When one transformer becomes stressed, neighbouring transformers on the same feeder may experience increased load.

TransformerGuard aims to identify these potential failure chains.

Beyond Risk Scores
A list of high-risk transformers is not enough.

TransformerGuard prioritizes maintenance using:

- Failure risk
- Farmers served
- Seasonal importance
- Backup availability

This helps DISCOM teams answer:

> **"Which transformer should we fix first?"**

## 🧠 AI Approach

The system uses temporal load/current patterns to identify abnormal transformer behaviour.

The planned architecture includes:

- Anomaly detection
- Transformer-level risk scoring
- Feeder-level risk propagation
- Human-impact-based prioritization
